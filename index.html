Crispy Chickpea & Roasted Veggie Bowls

View recipe ↗

Ingredients

For the crispy chickpeas & roasted veggies

For the bowls

For the tahini dressing

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 220°C / 425°F. Pat chickpeas completely dry.
  2. Toss chickpeas and chopped vegetables with oil, spices, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a baking sheet.
  3. Roast 25–30 minutes, tossing halfway, until the vegetables caramelize and chickpeas are crisp.
  4. Whisk tahini, citrus juice, honey and water until pourable.
  5. Build bowls with rice or quinoa, greens, roasted vegetables and chickpeas. Drizzle with tahini sauce and add avocado if desired.

Notes

For extra-crispy chickpeas, roast them on a separate tray. Swap any seasonal vegetables.

For the smashed cucumbers

For the spicy sesame dressing

To finish

Instructions

  1. Trim cucumbers, place on a cutting board and gently smack with the flat side of a knife or rolling pin until cracked.
  2. Cut or tear into bite-sized pieces. Toss with salt and let sit 5 minutes, then drain excess water.
  3. Mix garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, sugar and chili crisp.
  4. Toss cucumber with dressing; top with sesame seeds and green onion. Serve immediately.

Notes

Drain cucumbers before dressing so they stay crunchy. Add more chili crisp if you want it fiery.

Pantry staples · 15 min

Homemade Tahini

View recipe ↗

Ingredients

For the tahini

Instructions

  1. Toast sesame seeds over low to medium heat for 3–5 minutes, stirring frequently. Keep them pale golden, not brown.
  2. Cool the seeds, then blend in a food processor or powerful blender. Stop and scrape the sides.
  3. Add oil a little at a time only if needed to help the mixture become smooth.
  4. Transfer to a clean jar and refrigerate.

Notes

For a single noodle dish, you can blend toasted sesame directly into the sauce instead.
